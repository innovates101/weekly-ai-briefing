OpenAI Gives AI Agents a Credit Card

Overview

OpenAI just gave AI agents a credit card. ChatGPT Wallet lets autonomous agents complete purchases using ordinary debit and credit cards—no cryptocurrency required. This puts OpenAI directly in competition with Coinbase, Circle, and the payment rails that underpin global banking.

The Details

  • AI agents in ChatGPT can now autonomously execute card-based purchases on a user's behalf
  • The wallet runs on conventional card infrastructure, bypassing blockchain and stablecoin rails entirely
  • Direct competitor to crypto-native agent payment tools from Coinbase and Circle
  • Designed for fully autonomous online shopping—no human intervention required at checkout
  • OpenAI positioned as the authorization and intent layer between AI agents and commercial payment systems

Why It Matters

For banks and card issuers, the concept of cardholder presence just became complicated. When an AI agent initiates and authorizes a card transaction autonomously, existing fraud detection—trained on human behavioral signals—may flag legitimate agent activity or miss novel fraud patterns that look nothing like human shopping behavior.

The larger strategic risk: OpenAI wants to own the commercial intent layer. If agentic wallets scale, the moment a consumer decides to buy something happens inside a model, not at a checkout page. That shifts power away from card networks, processors, and issuers toward whoever controls the agent—and right now, that's OpenAI.
