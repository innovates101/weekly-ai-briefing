Google Turns Gemini Into a Full Business AI Agent

Overview

Google has transformed Gemini from a chatbot into a full agentic platform — one that plans complex tasks, delegates work to subagents, switches between AI models, and operates across enterprise apps. Business and enterprise customers get access first. TechCrunch has the full story.

The Details

  • Gemini Agent can autonomously plan and execute multi-step tasks, delegating subtasks to specialised subagents
  • It maintains its own persistent identity and can work across multiple connected business applications and systems
  • The rollout starts with business and enterprise customers ahead of any consumer release
  • Google is opening the framework to third-party models, including Anthropic's Claude
  • Positions Google directly against Microsoft Copilot and Salesforce Agentforce for enterprise workflow automation

Why It Matters

Banks already running on Google Workspace now have a native agentic layer that can orchestrate workflows across email, documents, and third-party systems — without a bespoke integration stack. For institutions evaluating agentic AI, this compresses the timeline from multi-year custom builds to a configurable deployment. The build-vs-buy calculus just shifted.

The multi-model architecture is the more important long-term signal. Financial institutions can route tasks to Claude or other specialised models from within the same agent framework. That flexibility matters for compliance and data residency — but it also raises hard questions about liability assignment, model governance, and audit trails when an agent chains together several models on a single workflow.
