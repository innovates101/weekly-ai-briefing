Why It Matters

AI pentest agents operate 24/7 — probing network perimeters, chaining vulnerabilities, and adapting attack strategies at machine speed. Security teams built to defend against human-paced threats are now facing a fundamentally different adversary. The open-source vector is the real alarm: sophisticated attack capability is now freely accessible, not gatekept by nation-state resources.
