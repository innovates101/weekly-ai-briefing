Banking's Core Just Got an AI Hardwire

Overview

Mambu just hardwired AI agents directly into core banking infrastructure. The cloud-native platform has launched Intelligent Core — connecting agentic AI directly to banking ledgers and payment systems, eliminating the middleware gap that has historically separated AI decision-making from actual transaction processing. Read more.

The Details

  • Mambu's Intelligent Core unites banking, payments, and agentic AI within a single platform layer
  • AI agents can now interact directly with banking ledgers — reading balances, triggering payments, and updating account records
  • This replaces the custom API and middleware workarounds banks have built to relay AI outputs into core systems
  • Multiple major fintech outlets — FF News, Fintech Singapore, and TechAfrica News — covered the launch simultaneously, signalling broad industry relevance
  • Mambu serves digital banks, challenger banks, and embedded finance providers globally as a cloud-native core banking platform

Why It Matters

Until now, AI in banking has largely operated as an advisory layer — flagging anomalies, generating recommendations, routing tickets. Intelligent Core changes the architecture: AI agents become direct participants in transactional workflows. For banks running Mambu, this could dramatically accelerate use cases like automated credit decisions, real-time fraud remediation, and agentic customer servicing — without custom middleware builds.

The governance implications are equally significant. Direct AI-to-ledger access raises the stakes for model risk management, explainability, and audit trails far beyond what most banks' current MRM frameworks anticipate. Regulators are already scrutinising agentic AI deployments; a system where AI agents write directly to financial ledgers will require banks to evidence human-in-the-loop controls, or demonstrate why they are not needed. This is the moment for risk and compliance teams to lead the architecture — not chase it.
