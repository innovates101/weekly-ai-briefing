TD Bank Bets on Proprietary AI to Win the Mortgage Race

Overview

TD Bank isn't waiting for an off-the-shelf answer to mortgage delays. Canada's second-largest bank is building a proprietary AI model to accelerate one of retail banking's most document-heavy processes — the mortgage application — according to Finextra Research. This is a strategic bet on AI as a competitive weapon, not a pilot.

The Details

  • TD Bank is developing a proprietary AI model to speed up mortgage application processing
  • Mortgage applications span weeks of document collection, income verification, credit review, and underwriting — each stage a target for AI compression
  • Building internally rather than licensing a vendor signals TD is treating AI as a source of durable competitive advantage
  • Proprietary development gives TD full control over model behavior, training data, and how customer data is handled throughout the process
  • As one of North America's largest banks, TD's deployment will attract close industry attention and set a benchmark others will feel pressure to match

Why It Matters

Mortgage approval speed is a genuine battleground for retail lending market share. Borrowers increasingly choose lenders on time-to-offer, not just rate. If TD's AI compresses a multi-week process by even a few days, it resets what applicants expect from every lender — and competitors that haven't started this work are already behind.

The harder implication is regulatory. Any AI that touches mortgage eligibility or processing sits squarely within model risk management frameworks and fair-lending obligations. Banks that lack mature model validation infrastructure will find replicating TD's move far more costly than the headline suggests. Speed is table stakes; building compliantly is where this race gets expensive.
