HSBC and Ant Test AI-Executed Payments on Tokenized Deposit Rails

Overview

HSBC and Ant Group's live pilot on the Jovay platform puts AI agents in charge of payments made with tokenized bank deposits — combining programmable money and autonomous AI in a single production-grade test. This is where the next payments stack is being built.

The Details

  • HSBC and Ant Group tested AI agents autonomously initiating and executing payment transactions using tokenized deposits on the Jovay blockchain platform.
  • Tokenized deposits are bank-issued digital representations of fiat currency on distributed ledger infrastructure — programmable, instant-settling, and auditable.
  • AI agents in the test operated without human intervention to execute payment instructions, a key feature of "agentic payments."
  • Jovay is designed for tokenized asset transactions, providing programmable settlement rails for condition-based payments.
  • The pilot combines two of banking's largest structural shifts — programmable money and autonomous AI — in a single live experiment at major-bank scale.

Why It Matters

This combination unlocks condition-based payments for trade finance, supply chain, and cross-border transactions that today take days of manual processing. Banks that build agent-ready tokenized payment infrastructure now will hold a structural edge in corporate treasury and transaction banking within three years.

The immediate compliance question: when an AI agent misroutes a tokenized payment, who is accountable? Banks need governance frameworks for agent-initiated transactions before these pilots scale. HSBC and Ant are setting the pace — expect competitors and regulators to pay very close attention to what comes next.
