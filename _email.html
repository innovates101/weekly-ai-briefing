Visa and Mastercard Are Writing the Rules for AI-Executed Payments

Overview

The major card networks aren't waiting for regulators to define agentic payments — they're doing it themselves. Visa and Mastercard have co-founded the Agentic Payments Alliance alongside fintech Rain, establishing the first major industry standards body dedicated to governing how AI agents transact.

The Details

  • Rain, Visa, and Mastercard have jointly formed the Agentic Payments Alliance — a new industry standards body for AI-executed payments.
  • The alliance will set protocols governing how AI agents authenticate, authorise, and complete payments autonomously on behalf of users.
  • Rain is a fintech building infrastructure specifically designed for agentic payment flows.
  • The move follows rising commercial interest in "agentic commerce" — AI that shops, pays, and transacts without manual confirmation at each step.
  • Separately, Synchrony Financial is already embedding its store cards inside ChatGPT, showing consumer-scale agentic payments are imminent.
  • No regulatory mandate drove this — it is industry self-organisation, ahead of rules that do not yet exist.

Why It Matters

The payment rails underpinning every bank, insurer, and fintech are about to be restructured around AI agents. The Agentic Payments Alliance means Visa and Mastercard are setting the interoperability standards that determine which institutions can participate in the next generation of commerce. Banks that sit out standards-setting will inherit rules designed by others — at costs they don't yet know.

For risk and compliance teams, this is also a liability clarification exercise. When an AI agent buys something on a customer's behalf and it goes wrong — who is responsible? The Alliance's standards will shape those answers. Institutions that engage now, rather than waiting for the final output, will have an outsized influence on frameworks they'll be living with for decades.
