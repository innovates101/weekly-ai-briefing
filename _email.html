Australia’s Big Banks Are Running AI Agent Swarms in Production

Overview

Westpac — Australia’s second-largest bank — has deployed a swarm of AI agents to assess loan applications using AWS cloud infrastructure. Rival NAB is simultaneously building agentic AI safeguards for its own customer rollout. Australia’s biggest banks aren’t piloting AI in lending anymore — they’re running it in production.

The Details

  • Westpac deployed a multi-agent “swarm” architecture on AWS to process loan applications end-to-end — not a single model, but coordinated agents handling distinct pipeline stages
  • The deployment spans both lending assessment and broader processing workflows, per reporting from Cyber Daily and the Australian Financial Review
  • National Australia Bank is separately beginning agentic AI testing for customer-facing products, with an explicit focus on building safeguards before any broad rollout
  • AWS serves as the infrastructure backbone for Westpac’s deployment — reinforcing cloud hyperscalers as the regulated AI delivery layer for major banks
  • Two of Australia’s four biggest banks making simultaneous agentic AI commitments in core functions signals sector-level adoption, not isolated experimentation
  • Loan assessment sits at the intersection of credit risk, responsible lending obligations, and consumer fairness — making this among the highest-stakes live AI deployments in any regulated industry

Why It Matters

When a bank the size of Westpac runs AI agent swarms on loan decisions in production, the compliance bar is being set in real time. Banks that haven’t started similar programmes are already behind the institutional frontier — and regulators in the UK, EU, and US will draw conclusions from what Australian banks are demonstrating now.

The Westpac/NAB contrast frames the strategic choice clearly: move fast in production or build safeguards first. Both approaches are defensible. But neither “pilot forever” nor “wait for perfect controls” is. The question has shifted from “should we automate lending with AI?” to “how fast, and with what oversight framework?”
