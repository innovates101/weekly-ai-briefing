Washington Just Showed Banks Their AI Vendor Can Go Dark

Overview

Trump's order forcing Anthropic to cut off foreign nationals from its top models — over fears of a jailbreak — has Macron lobbying for a G7 'trusted partners' carve-out. The lesson for every bank building on US AI: access can be switched off overnight, with zero notice.

The Details

  • The Trump administration ordered Anthropic to restrict foreign-national access to its most advanced models, Claude Mythos 5 and Claude Fable 5
  • Anthropic said the order followed concerns the US government had identified a jailbreak method affecting Fable 5
  • Anthropic disabled access to both models for affected users worldwide, with no advance warning to customers
  • French President Macron met Anthropic CEO Dario Amodei and OpenAI's Sam Altman to push for a "trusted partners" scheme letting allied nations retain access
  • Macron told G7 leaders the export limits were a "bad thing," warning that no government will keep building on US AI if access can be cut at any moment
  • G7 leaders are now discussing a formal framework for broadening allied access to frontier US models in the coming weeks

Why It Matters

Any bank, insurer, or asset manager running critical workflows on Claude or GPT just got a live demonstration of vendor concentration risk: a policy decision, not a technical outage, can sever access overnight. Risk and procurement teams need contingency plans for frontier-model dependency the same way they'd plan for a cloud-provider outage.

For non-US institutions specifically, this stacks geopolitical risk on top of regulatory and operational risk when an AI strategy leans on a single foreign vendor. Expect multi-model and sovereign-AI strategies to move from "nice to have" to board-level priority — and expect this fight to keep resurfacing.

1. AutoJack Attack Lets One Web Page Hijack AI Agent for Host Code Execution
The Hacker News
Microsoft researchers detailed AutoJack, an exploit chain in pre-release builds of AutoGen Studio that lets a malicious web page hijack an AI browsing agent and spawn code on the host via an unguarded local MCP endpoint. No exploitation in the wild; the stable release is unaffected.
🧠 Bold lead-in. Any institution piloting browsing agents should treat local agent endpoints as untrusted by default &mdash; this is exactly the vulnerability class regulators will ask about.
