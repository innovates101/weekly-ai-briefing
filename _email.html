  • Cracked a reduced-round ("weakened") form of AES — the encryption standard underpinning the vast majority of global financial transactions.

Why It Matters

Banks rely on AES for everything from ATM transaction encryption to SWIFT messaging and core banking data protection. Production AES-256 wasn't broken here — but the signal is unmistakable: AI can now systematically probe cryptographic algorithms at machine speed and scale. The era of automated cryptanalysis has arrived, and it will only accelerate as models become more capable.

More immediately alarming: HAWK is a post-quantum signature scheme that institutions have been quietly evaluating as a hedge against quantum computing threats. If AI can break next-generation cryptographic candidates before they even reach deployment, the post-quantum migration playbook needs urgent reassessment. CISOs should be running crypto algorithm inventories and stress-testing their roadmaps now — not after a breach forces the issue.
